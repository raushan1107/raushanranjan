07 — FAQ

Frequently asked
questions.

Who is Raushan Ranjan?

Raushan Ranjan, known to learners as Raushan Sir, is a Microsoft Certified Trainer (MCT) and AI and cloud developer based in Noida, India, and the creator of RR Skillverse, a free learning platform. He has trained 4,000+ professionals across 175+ enterprise batches in 25+ countries, holds 12 active certifications, and builds AI agents and full-stack solutions on Azure, Power Platform and .NET.

Who is Raushan Sir?

Raushan Sir is what learners call Raushan Ranjan, a Microsoft Certified Trainer from Noida, India, and the creator of RR Skillverse (rrskillverse.in), a free learning platform for Azure, AI engineering, Power BI, Power Platform and .NET.

What is RR Skillverse?

RR Skillverse (rrskillverse.in) is a free, non-commercial learning platform created by Raushan Ranjan. It brings together his notes, handbooks, blogs and AI tools, with Axiom, an AI learning assistant, under the tagline Learn · Build · Grow.

What is RR Education?

RR Education is the coaching centre Raushan Ranjan started in 2014 with a single student. He taught physics and mathematics there for 7+ years, including NEET/JEE support, before moving into enterprise technology training in 2022.

What is RRCareerOS?

RRCareerOS is Raushan Ranjan's personal, evidence-based career platform: it matches job descriptions against his career record with explanations, writes tailored resumes in which every claim is checked against evidence, plans learning for skill gaps, and answers questions through a grounded chat assistant. It runs on Azure.

What certifications does Raushan Ranjan hold?

He is a Microsoft Certified Trainer (since August 2023) and holds 12 active certifications: 10 Microsoft certifications, Azure AI Apps and Agents Developer (AI-103), Azure AI Cloud Developer (AI-200), Fabric Analytics Engineer (DP-600), Azure Developer (AZ-204), Power Platform Functional Consultant (PL-200), Power Platform Developer (PL-400), Power BI Data Analyst (PL-300), Azure Data Fundamentals, Power Platform Fundamentals and Azure Fundamentals; the MCT; and CompTIA Data+. He has passed AZ-305 and is completing Azure Solutions Architect Expert (AZ-104), with AI-901 and AB-410 scheduled.

What does Raushan Ranjan teach?

Azure AI engineering and generative AI, Azure development and architecture, Power BI and Microsoft Fabric, Power Platform and Copilot Studio, GitHub Copilot, C# and .NET (ASP.NET Core, Blazor), system design, and C++ with Qt/QML, OpenGL and Vulkan.

How many professionals has Raushan Ranjan trained?
